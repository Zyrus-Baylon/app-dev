## About the Series

**Pokémon** is a franchise about trainers who travel the world to catch, train, and battle creatures called **Pokémon**. It started in **1996** with the Game Freak video games *Pokémon Red and Green* in Japan, and later grew into an anime, trading cards, movies, and more!

> My favorite part is the sense of adventure: every route has something new to discover.
